# Exhibition demo runbook

## Main live demo
1. Show the dashboard status panel and confirm local model names are visible.
2. Disconnect internet while keeping the private local Wi-Fi network up. Ask a short English question and an everyday Andhra Telugu question by voice.
3. Save a harmless fact in memory, restart the laptop app, and retrieve it in a relevant question.
4. Ask a visual question aloud. Confirm dashboard camera control and the physical switch are enabled; observe the camera indicator while the board captures one frame.
5. Show the vision answer and separate OCR text. Speak only the short answer; the complete response remains visible in the dashboard.
6. Turn dashboard camera control off and demonstrate capture refusal. Then turn the physical switch off and demonstrate capture refusal again.
7. Show the offline test checklist and feature status honestly.

## Rehearsal requirements
- Perform the complete demo successfully three times before exhibition day.
- Rehearse with internet disconnected.
- Keep a backup recording clearly labelled as a backup, never as the live run.
- Do not capture visitors without their permission.
- Do not claim a feature worked unless the result was observed on the actual hardware.
