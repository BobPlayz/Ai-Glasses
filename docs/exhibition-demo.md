# Exhibition demo runbook

## Main live demo
1. Show the dashboard status panel and confirm local model names are visible.
2. Disconnect internet, keep the private local Wi-Fi network up, and show the local model still answers.
3. Ask one short English question and one everyday Andhra Telugu question.
4. Save a harmless fact in memory, restart the laptop app, and retrieve the fact in a relevant question.
5. Ask a visual question. Confirm the physical switch is enabled, observe the hardware indicator, and let the board capture one frame.
6. Show the model name and answer. Keep long answers as text; speak short answers only if the local Telugu/English voice has passed rehearsals.
7. Turn the physical switch off and demonstrate that capture is refused.
8. Show the offline test checklist and feature status honestly.

## Rehearsal requirements
- Perform the complete demo successfully three times before exhibition day.
- Rehearse with internet disconnected.
- Keep a backup recording clearly labelled as a backup, never as the live run.
- Do not capture visitors without their permission.
- Do not claim microphone streaming if only camera transport has been integrated.
