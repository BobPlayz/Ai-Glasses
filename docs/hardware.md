No board has been purchased or verified.

Candidate class: ESP32-S3 board with camera and microphone included, Wi-Fi, USB programming, and documented firmware support. Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense is a candidate, not yet selected.

Before purchase, verify exact listing/revision, camera and expansion board inclusion, antenna, microphone support, USB programming, shipping date, firmware examples, and total cost including frame mount, switch, indicator, wiring, and audio output.

Prototype on USB power first. Do not improvise lithium battery wiring or charging. The physical privacy switch must actually disable capture.

Total budget: ₹3,000–₹6,000. Do not buy until delivery and all required parts are checked.
